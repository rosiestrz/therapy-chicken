A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you join the flock.

What is “chicken therapy”?

It’s the name of Psi Beta’s crochet chicken project: handmade companions and kits that invite creativity and connection. These chickens are comfort crafts, not a clinical treatment or a substitute for mental health care.

Do I need to know how to crochet?

The kit includes a pattern booklet to guide your project. If you’re new to crochet, check with Psi Beta about the skill level and any available help before ordering. You can also choose a ready-made chicken.

How do I order or donate?

Use either order button to see the original flyer and its ordering QR code. Suggested donations are $15+ for a finished therapy chicken and $10+ for a Chicken Therapy Kit. Orders and donations are handled through the club’s ordering destination.

Are there any care or safety considerations?

Follow the kit booklet for assembly and care. Safety eyes and other small parts can pose a choking hazard; keep them away from young children and pets.
